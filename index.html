Quick guide

Draw with Apple Pencil. Once the Pencil is used, your fingers only move and zoom, so your palm won't leave marks.

Brush types: pick the Brush, then choose Ink, Smoke, Airbrush, Charcoal, Watercolour or Glow in the panel on the right. Press harder with the Pencil for a wider stroke. Each type remembers its own size and opacity. Glow looks best on a dark background.

Gestures: pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to move, two-finger tap to undo, three-finger tap to redo.

Frame by frame: draw, then tap Next blank to move on and draw the next drawing. Onion skin shows the previous frame in red and the next one in green. Tap Options next to Onion to show it for just the current layer, or draw it in front of everything.

Copy and paste frames: tap Frames (or long-press a frame). While the menu is open, tap a frame, then tap another frame to pick every frame in between. Tap Copy, then tap the frame where they should go and tap Paste over to replace frames, or Insert before / Insert after to add them in between. Switch to All layers to copy every layer at once.

Hold a drawing longer: tap Hold +1 to add a frame after the current one.

Tweens: draw on frame 1. Tap a later frame and tap Keyframe. Move, resize or reshape the drawing there with the Select tool. Go back to frame 1 and tap Tween. The frames in between are filled in automatically.

Layers: use the buttons at the top-left of the timeline. Tap a layer's name to choose it, double-tap to rename. The eye hides it and the lock protects it.

Images: tap Image at the top to add a PNG or photo to the current layer and frame. You can also paste or drag one in. Move, resize and rotate it with the Select tool, and tween it like a drawing.

Saving: everything saves automatically on this iPad. Use Export → Project file for a backup.

Keyboard: B brush, N pen, E eraser, V select, L line, R rectangle, O ellipse, K fill, H hand, Space play, , and . step frames, ⌘Z undo. With frames picked: ⌘C copy, ⌘X cut, ⌘V paste over, ⇧⌘V insert, ⌘D duplicate, Delete removes them.
